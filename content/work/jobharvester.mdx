# The brief

Job boards are noisy. Most candidates waste hours opening the same
listing on five different sites, copy-pasting descriptions, and
guessing which roles actually match their skills. I wanted a single
feed that ranks, dedupes, and explains why each role fits — or
doesn't.

## What I built

- A scraper fleet targeting the top 20 job boards, with polite
  rate-limiting and a circuit-breaker for sites that ban aggressively
- An embeddings-based dedup layer that collapses near-identical
  listings across sources
- A skill-gap analyzer that compares a candidate's resume to the
  posting's required skills and surfaces the gap
- A daily-ranked feed with explainers ("strong match: 7/8 skills",
  "weak match: 2/8 skills", "location mismatch")

## What I learned

The hardest problem wasn't matching candidates to roles — it was
matching listings to listings. Same job, twenty boards, twenty slightly
different descriptions. The dedup layer was where I learned that
embeddings are a great first pass but never quite enough on their own.

I paused the project when I joined EPAM and shifted focus to the
agent platform, but the matching logic is what inspired how we
rank documents inside the RCA subsystem today.
